03 / UNDER THE HOOD

The math doesn't lie.

SKIPS = floor((A − 0.75H) ÷ 0.75)

For a 75% requirement. A = classes attended, H = classes held. The result is rounded down to a whole class. Your college may calculate attendance by subject or course component, so check its official policy.
